# Gym Owner Cold Outreach Plan

## Goal

Land a paid website project with a local gym owner by demonstrating clear value, not just skill.

---

## Research First (before any contact)

- [ ] Find their current site — note speed, mobile UX, outdated design, missing features
- [ ] Check Google Maps reviews — what do members praise or complain about?
- [ ] Look at their social media — active? What do they post?
- [ ] Identify one concrete problem the site has (slow load, no booking CTA, ugly on mobile)
- [ ] Screenshot it for reference

---

## Channel Priority

1. **Instagram DM** — gyms live here, owners check it
2. **Facebook Page DM** — backup if IG fails
3. **In-person visit** — strongest, use if above fail
4. **Email** — lowest open rate, last resort

---

## First Message Script (IG/FB DM)

> Hey [Name], I've been following [Gym Name] for a bit — love the energy you bring to [specific thing they post about].
>
> I'm a web developer and I noticed [one specific problem: e.g., "your site doesn't have an online booking option" / "loads slow on mobile"]. I put together a quick idea of what a fix could look like.
>
> Would you be open to a 15-min call this week? No pitch, just want to show you what I'm thinking.

**Rules:**

- Specific compliment, not generic
- Name ONE problem — not a list
- Low-commitment ask (15 min, not "hire me")
- No price mentioned yet

---

## Follow-Up Sequence

| Day | Action                                                                                           |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0   | Send first message                                                                               |
| 3   | Follow up if no reply — "Just bumping this up in case it got buried"                             |
| 7   | Final follow-up — share a mock screenshot or quick Loom video of their site with your suggestion |
| 14  | Move on, try next gym                                                                            |

---

## The Call (15-min frame)

1. Ask about their business pain first — "What's the #1 thing you wish more members did after finding you online?"
2. Show their current site vs. a competitor's or a mockup
3. Propose a small, specific scope — not "full redesign", but "membership page + booking CTA"
4. Anchor price to their pain ("if this gets you 3 more signups a month at $50/mo, it pays for itself in weeks")

---

## Offer Structure

**Starter Package** — $500–$800

- Landing page redesign
- Mobile-optimized
- Contact / booking CTA
- Google Maps embed + hours

**Full Site** — $1,200–$2,500

- Multi-page site
- Class schedule
- Membership info
- Blog / testimonials
- Basic SEO

**Retainer (optional upsell)** — $100–$200/mo

- Hosting management
- Monthly content updates
- Performance monitoring

---

## Objections and Responses

| Objection                  | Response                                                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "We already have a site"   | "I know — I actually looked at it and noticed [X]. That's why I reached out."        |
| "We don't have budget"     | "What's a realistic budget? I can scope something to fit."                           |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. Want me to send a one-page summary so you have it when you're ready?" |
| "We do it ourselves"       | "Respect that. If you ever want a second pair of eyes, I'm around."                  |
